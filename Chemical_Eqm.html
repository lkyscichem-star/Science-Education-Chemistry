💡 Graph Observation Guide & Hints

How to read the Spikes and Slopes at t=30s:
  • Blue Line = [N₂] (Initial: 20 cm³), Red Line = [H₂] (Initial: 60 cm³), and Green Line = [NH₃] (Initial: 40 cm³)
  • Concentration Spikes: If you intentionally add a specific gas, you will see exactly one sharp vertical spike at t=30s for that species only. This represents the instant change in concentration.
  • Pressure Spikes: If you increase pressure (or halve the volume), you will see three simultaneous upward spikes at t=30s because decreasing the volume compresses all gases equally.
  • Temperature Transitions: Adjusting the temperature causes zero vertical spikes. All three lines transition into smooth, steady curves starting from t=30s.
  • Catalyst Impact: Changing the catalyst yields no changes on the concentration graph because a catalyst accelerates both forward and backward reactions equally.
