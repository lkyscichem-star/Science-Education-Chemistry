Common HKDSE Naming & Formula Traps
  • Monoatomic (-ide) vs Polyatomic (-ate / -ite):

    Nitride is N³⁻, Nitrite is NO₂⁻, Nitrate is NO₃⁻.

  • Brackets Rule in Ionic Compounds:

    Use brackets ONLY when more than one polyatomic ion is present! E.g. Mg(OH)₂, but NaOH (NOT Na(OH)).

  • Roman Numerals for Variable Valency:

    Transition metals need Roman numerals: Iron(II) Fe²⁺, Iron(III) Fe³⁺, Copper(II) Cu²⁺, Silver(I) Ag⁺.

HKDSE Chemistry Master Advice

"In HKDSE Chemistry, all main group ions (Group I, II, III metals and halide/oxide anions) are COLOURLESS in aqueous solution!"

Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺, Zn²⁺, Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, SO₄²⁻, CO₃²⁻, NH₄⁺ ➔ ALL COLOURLESS!

Only specific transition metal ions and complex oxoanions (MnO₄⁻, Cr₂O₇²⁻) impart distinct colors to aqueous solutions. Memorize those 8 colored ions and you win all color questions in HKDSE Paper 1!
