Part D1: HKDSE Qualitative Analysis Key Concepts & Common Pitfalls Exam Marking Keys

Avoid common errors that cost marks in HKDSE Chemistry structured qualitative analysis questions.

⚠️ Pitfall 1 Tollens' vs 2,4-DNPH Reagent

2,4-DNPH tests broadly for any carbonyl group (C=O), giving orange/red precipitates with both aldehydes and ketones. In contrast, Tollens' reagent specifically tests for aldehydes because aldehydes have a terminal hydrogen atom on the carbonyl carbon allowing oxidation to carboxylic acid derivatives.

⚠️ Pitfall 2 Role of Acidification with dil. HNO₃

In haloalkane testing, after heating with NaOH(alc) to hydrolyze the C-X bond, dilute HNO₃ MUST be added before AgNO₃(aq). This neutralizes excess hydroxide ions (OH⁻). Unneutralized OH⁻ reacts with Ag⁺ to form a brown precipitate of Ag₂O, resulting in false positive observations.

⚠️ Pitfall 3 Electrophilic Addition vs Substitution

Alkenes decolorize Br₂ in organic solvent rapidly even in the dark via electrophilic addition across C=C double bonds. In contrast, alkanes require ultraviolet (UV) light to undergo free radical substitution with bromine.

⚠️ Pitfall 4 Oxidation Behavior of Alcohols

Primary alcohols are oxidized to aldehydes and then carboxylic acids. Secondary alcohols are oxidized to ketones. Both turn acidified K₂Cr₂O₇ from orange to green (Cr³⁺). Tertiary alcohols resist oxidation under standard lab conditions and remain orange.

Q1 Model Solution (3 Marks):

Reagent: Br₂ in CH₂Cl₂ (organic solvent) in dark [1M]. Observation for Hex-1-ene: Orange-brown solution rapidly decolorizes [1M]. Observation for Hexane: Solution remains orange-brown [1M].

Q2 Model Solution (3 Marks):

2,4-DNPH reacts with both C=O groups forming orange ppt [1M]. Appropriate reagent: Tollens' reagent [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺, warmed [1M]. Propanal forms a silver mirror while Propanone gives no observable change [1M].

Q3 Model Solution (3 Marks):

Add NaHCO₃(aq) to both test tubes [1M]. Ethanoic acid reacts giving rapid effervescence of gas that turns limewater milky (CO₂) [1M]. Ethanol shows no effervescence / no observable change [1M].
